Questions, answered

Is Bagay-Bagay really free?

Yes. Posting, requesting, and trading cost nothing. Selling isn’t allowed, so everyone stays on equal footing.

How do I stay safe when meeting someone?

Meet in public places like barangay halls or plazas, bring a friend if you can, and read each member’s profile and past thank-you notes first.

What can I post?

Clothes, books, appliances, toys, furniture, kitchenware, and more. Items must be clean, working, and legal to share.
